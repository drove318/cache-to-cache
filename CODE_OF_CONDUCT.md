# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — the maintainers and the contributors of C2C — pledge to make
participation in our project and our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, whatever the age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

C2C is a wire between models; a wire works when the people around it
work together. We want the whole of it — the paper reproduced, the
tests passed, the love made — without anyone made to feel less than a
peer.

## Standards for behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other community members;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experience — the
  beginner's question is often the expert's blind spot;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism;
- correcting, in the code, what was wrong; correcting, in the record,
  what was misreported; and reporting, never in the open, what is
  security (see SECURITY.md).

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insulting, or derogatory comments, and personal or
  political attacks where they are not on the subject;
- publication, private or public, of another's information without
  leave (the "doxing" of an adversary);
- sustained disruption of a thread, a review, or a release;
- any conduct that, in the reasonable judgement of the maintainers,
  would make the project unsafe for a newcomer to join or for a
  maintainer to merge.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the issue tracker, the pull
requests, the discussions, the commits, the releases, the chat rooms —
and in public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the maintainers at `158539850+drove318@users.noreply.github.com`. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Community leaders who do not follow or enforce the Code in good faith
may face temporary or permanent removal from the project's leadership,
as determined by the other leaders.
